Terms of Service

1. Who we are

Cor Studios Analyzer (the "App") is operated by Cor Studios, a sole proprietorship ("Cor Studios", "we"). Contact: corstudiosai@gmail.com.

The App is a private analytics tool used by Cor Studios to analyze its own social media accounts and to publish its own videos to them. It is not offered to the general public.

2. What we collect from TikTok

Only when the TikTok account owner connects the App by logging in on TikTok and approving the requested permissions, we receive through TikTok's official Login Kit and Display API:

Permission (scope)Data
user.info.basicOpen ID (an app-specific account identifier) and display name
user.info.statsFollower, following, total likes and video counts
video.listFor each public video: ID, title, description/caption, posting date, duration, share link, cover image link, whether it is labelled AI-generated, and view, like, comment and share counts

We never see or store your TikTok password. You enter it only on TikTok's own website. We do not receive private videos, direct messages, or the data of your followers or viewers.

3. How we use it

Solely to show the account owner how their own posts perform over time (for example which topics, formats and posting times get more views) and to help plan future content. We do not use it for advertising, profiling of individuals, or any purpose unrelated to the owner's own analytics.

4. Where it is stored and how it is protected

  • Data is stored in a database on a personal computer controlled by the Cor Studios operator. It is not currently hosted on a cloud service or remote server. If that changes, we will update this policy before any TikTok data is stored in the new location.
  • TikTok access and refresh tokens are kept in a separate file restricted by operating-system permissions to the App's user account. They are not stored in the database, not written to logs, and not committed to source control.
  • The database file and its folder are restricted to the App's user account.
  • The App's web interface is only reachable from the computer it runs on, and request logs do not record login codes or other URL parameters.
  • All communication with TikTok uses HTTPS.

No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect this data.

4a. Other platforms: imported files

The App can also analyze performance files (such as CSV exports) that the account owner downloads from Instagram, YouTube or TikTok and chooses to import. These files are processed in the same way and are covered by this policy.

4b. Instagram and YouTube connections

When the account owner chooses to connect an Instagram professional account or a YouTube channel, they sign in on Instagram (Meta) or on Google and approve the permissions shown there. The App never sees or stores the password. The permissions it asks for are: on Instagram, to see the account's basic profile and to publish content to it; on YouTube, to see the channel's name and ID and to upload videos to it.

  • What the App accesses: the account or channel ID, its name or username, the permissions that were granted, and, for a post the App itself publishes, that post's processing status, ID and link. It does not read the account's other posts, comments, messages, followers or statistics.
  • What the App stores (only on the operator's own computer): the connection record (account ID, name, permissions, expiry date, status); the access credentials (an access token and, for Google, a refresh token), kept in a private, permission-restricted file and never in the database, in logs or on this website; the drafts the account owner creates (the video file, title, captions and chosen accounts); and the results of publishing (post ID and link, status, error messages and an activity log that contains no credentials).
  • What the App publishes: only a video, with the title, description or caption the owner wrote, to the account the owner chose, and only when the owner explicitly tells the App to publish it. It does not post automatically, does not edit or delete existing posts, does not send messages and does not post on its own.
  • Automatic background activity: to keep a connection working the App from time to time renews the access credentials with Instagram or Google. It does nothing else with these accounts in the background.
  • Instagram video hosting: Instagram requires a video to be fetched from a web address. For the duration of a publish the App makes the chosen video available at a temporary, randomly generated address that works only for that one file, and removes it when the publish ends. If the operator uses a tunnel service to make that address reachable from the internet, the video passes through that service during that time.
  • Sharing: credentials, videos and captions are sent only to Instagram (Meta) or Google/YouTube, as needed to carry out an action the owner requested. They are not sold or shared with anyone else.
  • Google and YouTube: the App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. By using the YouTube connection you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Disconnecting and deleting: choosing "Disconnect" in the App deletes the stored credentials and the connection record, and for YouTube also asks Google to revoke the App's access. You can remove the App yourself at any time: on Instagram under Settings → Apps and websites, and on Google at myaccount.google.com → Security → Third-party access. Deleting an unpublished draft in the App deletes its video file, captions and log (only the item's title may remain as an archived entry in the App's content library). Records of what was already published are kept as history unless you ask us to delete them: email the address above and we will complete eligible requests within 30 days (the backup exception in section 7 applies).
  • What the App does not do: it does not currently read performance statistics from Instagram or YouTube through their APIs (only files you import), and it does not post to TikTok. If that changes, this policy will be updated first.

The operator may also manually record publicly visible statistics of other creators' posts for benchmarking. This is typed in by the operator and is not obtained through TikTok's API.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share TikTok data with third parties.

We do not automatically send TikTok account data to external AI services (such as Claude or OpenAI) or to any other outside service. We will not enable any such sharing without first updating this policy and obtaining the account owner's explicit permission.

The App's dashboard loads a public charting library from a content delivery network (cdn.jsdelivr.net) in the owner's browser; no TikTok data is sent to it. We may disclose data only if legally required.

6. Retention

Video statistics and periodic snapshots of them are kept for as long as the account owner uses the App, so that performance can be compared over time, until it is deleted at your request or by us. Tokens are kept only while the account is connected and are deleted when you disconnect. When you disconnect you can choose to delete the imported statistics at the same time; if you choose to keep them, they are kept until you request deletion as described below. Retention is also subject to any retention or deletion requirements that apply to us under TikTok's developer terms or the law.

7. Disconnecting and deleting data

  • Disconnect: choosing "Disconnect" in the App asks TikTok to revoke the App's access and immediately deletes the stored tokens and the connection record, so the App can no longer access the account. The App then asks whether you also want to permanently delete the videos and statistics it imported from TikTok; if you choose yes, they are deleted immediately. If TikTok cannot be reached, the local tokens are still deleted and you can remove the App in TikTok's settings.
  • Revoke at TikTok: you can also remove the App's access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps and services (menu names may vary).
  • Delete imported data: if you disconnected without choosing to delete the statistics already imported, you can still ask us to erase them. Email us at the address above to request deletion of all data obtained from your TikTok account. We will permanently delete the imported videos, statistics, snapshots and account totals from our database, and we will complete eligible requests within 30 days (subject to the backup exception below). We may need to confirm that you control the account first.
  • Backups and copies: we do not intentionally keep separate copies of this database. However, because it is stored on an ordinary personal computer, copies may exist in general-purpose backups or system-level copies of that computer (for example full-disk or cloud-synced backups) if such tools are in use. When fulfilling a deletion request we delete the live database records and any separate copies we know of; copies inside general backups are removed when those backups are overwritten or expire under their normal schedule, which may take longer than 30 days. We do not restore deleted data from backups into the App. Data that we are legally required to keep is the only other exception.

8. Your choices and rights

You may ask us what data we hold from your account, ask us to correct or delete it, or withdraw your permission at any time using the steps above. Depending on where you live you may have additional rights under local law; contact us to exercise them.

The App is not directed to children, and TikTok requires users to meet its minimum age. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
